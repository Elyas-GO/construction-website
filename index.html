Architecture, Construction
& Infrastructure

We offer a comprehensive range of construction services, ensuring every project is executed to the highest standard from foundation to finishing.

01. Consultancy
Expert construction consultancy covering feasibility studies, site assessment, design review, BOQ preparation, and cost estimation for all project types.
02. Residential Project
Single-family homes, villas, apartments, and G+1 to G+4 residential buildings constructed to ECA standards with quality finishes and on-time delivery.

Architectural design, structural engineering, and detailed drawings for all building types. We work with licensed architects to deliver practical, attractive designs.

Comprehensive project planning including scheduling, resource allocation, procurement planning, and compliance with Ethiopian building codes and ECA regulations.
